Your key is
a password made
of metal.
Inside,
tiny pin pairs
sit on springs
and the top
pins block the
cylinder.
Wrong key.
One pin stops
just short of
the line and
it jams.
The right key
lifts every
pin to exactly
the right height
so every gap
lines up on
one line.
Click.
The door swings
open.
Five pins,
10 heights each,
almost 80,000
possible keys
and only yours
fits.
Which is why.
