## 3. Scene-by-scene findings (vidIQ watched 4 videos)
- **Hook (all 4):** the action is already happening at 0.0 s — no intro. Narrated hits open with a bold claim
  or a second-person "If you…" line *over* the action: "If you try to pull open a security tag…" (Zack D),
  "You can't escape modern handcuffs." (98x). Silent hits open with the key already entering a cutaway.
- **Character:** Zack D uses a full character mini-story with a comic consequence (karma). The 98x 3D video only
  shows a hand to dramatise the danger. The silent giants show no character at all.
- **Problem before solution (98x):** first shows what goes wrong without the mechanism (wrist pinched, shimmed),
  then reveals the mechanism. Creates stakes.
- **Reveal method:** colour-coded cutaway (yellow / blue / orange parts) + x-ray overlays + macro close-ups.
- **Pacing:** a new shot every 2–3 s (9 cuts in 29 s for Zack D); camera orbits and swoops; ~150 words/minute.
- **Captions:** 2–4 words, bottom-centre, bold with a dark/light stroke; yellow on the 98x video.
- **Audio:** clear male explainer voice, **quiet rhythmic music bed**, crisp mechanical clicks, whooshes on cuts.
  Silent hits: pure mechanical ASMR only.
- **Ending:** 98x video ends on "And that's why…" while the camera snaps back to the exact first frame → loop.
  Silent hits end on the exact start state (perfect loop, 150–200% average view).
- **Comparison hook (34M):** "Normal Key vs Bump Key" on screen — one clean contrast is enough.

## 4. The framework we use (v6)
1. 0.0 s: hand/character already doing the action + bold second-person line ("If you…").
2. Problem first: show it failing (wrong key jams, character struggles) before explaining.
3. "The reason is hiding inside" → whip into a colour-coded cutaway.
4. Explain in 3–4 beats, one shot per beat, camera always moving, 2–4-word captions.
5. Payoff: the right key — gaps line up — click — door opens (character win).
6. Twist/consequence line about the tiny difference (less than a millimetre).
7. End on "and that's why…" with the camera back on frame 1 → seamless loop.
8. Bonus every time: a 6–10 s silent "Right key vs Wrong key" cutaway loop (no voice, mechanical sounds only).
