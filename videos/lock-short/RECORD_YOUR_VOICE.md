# Record your voice for the lock Short

Read this **exactly as written**: the video times every shot to these words, so the same words in the same order
make the picture line up automatically. Aim for about 27–30 seconds. Energetic and natural, like telling a friend.

> If you put the wrong key in a lock, it won't turn.
> And the reason is hiding inside.
> Every lock hides tiny pins, stacked in pairs, pushed down by springs.
> The top pins block the cylinder, until the right key lifts every pair to exactly the right height.
> Then every gap lines up on one line, and *click*, it turns.
> The wrong key misses by less than a millimetre.
> So one tiny pin is all it takes to lock you out.
> And that's why…

## Tips
- Quiet room, no fan or music. Phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- Start talking right away. Don't worry about small pauses: I cut them out automatically.
- One clean take is enough. If you trip on a word, just restart the whole thing.
- Any format is fine (Vocaroo, voice memo, m4a, mp3, wav).
