# Lock Short v6 script (framework: RESEARCH.md §4) — 79 words, ~30 s
If you put the wrong key in a lock, it won't turn.
And the reason is hiding inside.
Every lock hides pins, stacked in pairs, pushed down by springs.
The top pins block the cylinder…
until the right key lifts every pair to exactly the right height.
Then every gap lines up on one line,
and click, it turns.
The wrong key misses by less than a millimetre.
That's why one tiny pin can lock you out…
and that's why — (loops to) if you put the wrong key in a lock, it won't turn.
