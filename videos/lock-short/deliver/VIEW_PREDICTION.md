- **A character mini-story** (stickman jams the key, panics, wins, gets locked out). This is Zack D's format; going by
  their titles and descriptions, the 14 small-channel copies are plain cutaways (I couldn't watch them to confirm).
- **Hook in the first second:** voice and action on frame 1, problem shown before the explanation.
- **Retention:** 26.9 s, no pause over 0.25 s, a new shot every ~2 s, loop ending (re-watches push the "% viewed" over 100%).
- Correct physics, labelled parts, competitor-style captions.

## Why it could still flop
- **New channel, zero subscribers:** YouTube first shows a Short to a small test group (a few hundred people). It only
  spreads if most of them don't swipe away in the first 2 seconds and many watch to the end.
- **Crowded topic:** at least 14 look-alike lock Shorts in the last 7 weeks, and Zack D's 16.5M video already owns it.
  One of them even uses our working title — **don't use "How a Lock Knows Your Key".**
- One video is a coin flip. Channels in section B that broke out had usually posted several before the hit.
