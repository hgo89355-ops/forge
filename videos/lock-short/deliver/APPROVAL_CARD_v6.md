## What changed (from the research on the top lock / mechanism Shorts)
- **Hook in the first second:** close-up of our guide's hand pushing the wrong key into his front-door lock,
  voice on frame 1: "If you put the wrong key in a lock, it won't turn." (problem first, the way the 98x handcuff
  Short and the 26M-view Zack D. Short open).
- **Mini-story with the character:** he jams the key, panics → "the reason is hiding inside" → camera dives
  into the lock → colour-coded cutaway → right key, gaps line up, *click* → the door swings open and he cheers.
- **Twist + karma ending:** "So one tiny pin is all it takes to lock you out" → back at the door, wrong key,
  sheepish "oops" → "And that's why…" lands on the same shot as frame 1, so the video loops.
- **Character v2:** the guide now has a human shape (real head with jaw, ear and hair, shaped shirt with sleeves,
  tapered arms and legs, hands with a thumb, sneakers); no more box body. His hand is placed on the key with
  arm maths, so he really holds it.
- **Captions moved to the competitor standard:** white, thick black outline, keywords in yellow, 1–3 words,
  lower third. Our white label boxes and blue title tag stay (our brand).
- **Shorter:** 26.9 s. The winners we studied run 29–34 s (narrated) or 5–12 s (silent loops), so we're in range.
