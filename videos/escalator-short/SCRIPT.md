# Escalator Steps Secretly Flip Upside Down — Short (draft v1, Zack-style pacing)

Target 26–32 s · ~80 words · speech from frame 1 · pauses < 0.25 s · ends on a line that loops to the first.
Story: our guide rides up, watches the top step vanish into the floor, peeks under the comb plate (shocked),
follows one step around the red drive gear and back underneath, upside down; it flips back at the bottom.

## Script (78 words)
Escalator steps secretly flip upside down.
Every step has two pairs of wheels, and each pair rolls on its own hidden track.
On the slope, the tracks stay parallel, so your step stays flat.
At the top they come together, and the steps flatten into a floor.
Then a gear, driven by the motor, pulls the chain around,
and your step rides back underneath, upside down.
About half the steps are always down there.
At the bottom, it flips back over, because…

(loops to) "escalator steps secretly flip upside down."
