## Length: ~5–6 minutes (owner: shorter is fine; don't pad)
A new channel lives or dies on *percentage watched*. A tight 5½-minute video that people finish beats a padded 9-minute
one. The Short only covers one idea (pins + shear line), so there's real material for ~5–6 minutes without filler. If a
chapter feels thin when we build it, we cut it — never stretch.
