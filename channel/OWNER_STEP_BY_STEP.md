# Your step-by-step: from zero to the first Short live (≈ 45 minutes)

You do the parts that need **you** (your phone, your passwords, your decisions). The Claude Chrome extension does
everything else at the same time (prompt: `CHANNEL_SETUP_PROMPT.md`). Tick each box as you go.

---

## PHASE 1 — Account security (you, 10 min, before anything else)
Why: new channels get hacked a lot, and YouTube trusts accounts with 2-step verification more.

1. ☐ On your computer, open **Chrome** and sign in to the Gmail account that will own the channel.
   - Tip: if this is your personal Gmail, that's fine — the channel will have its own name (Phase 2).
2. ☐ Go to **myaccount.google.com/security**.
3. ☐ Find **"2-Step Verification"** → click it → **Get started**.
4. ☐ Enter your phone number → choose **Text message** → type the code you receive → **Turn on**.
5. ☐ On the same security page, click **"Backup codes"** → **Get backup codes** → write them on paper and keep it
   somewhere safe. (Don't save them in a file on the computer and never send them to anyone — including me.)
6. ☐ Still on the security page, check **Recovery phone** and **Recovery email** are filled in. Add them if empty.

## PHASE 2 — Create the channel (you, 5 min)
7. ☐ Go to **youtube.com** → click your picture (top right) → **Settings** → **"Add or manage your channel(s)"** →
   **"Create a channel"** (this makes a *brand* channel with its own name — better than using your personal name;
   you can add helpers later).
8. ☐ Name: **Hidden Engineering** (or the name you chose). Tick the box → **Create**.
9. ☐ Go to **youtube.com/verify** → choose your country → **Text me the code** → enter your phone → type the code →
   **Submit**. This unlocks custom thumbnails, longer videos and live features.

## PHASE 3 — Let the extension do the setup (it works, you do Phase 4 meanwhile)
10. ☐ Download **avatar.png** and **banner.png** (I sent them) → make sure they're in your **Downloads** folder.
11. ☐ Open **studio.youtube.com** (make sure the top-right picture shows the *Hidden Engineering* channel, not your
    personal one — switch via your picture → **Switch account** if needed).
12. ☐ Open the **Claude extension** → paste everything from `CHANNEL_SETUP_PROMPT.md` → send.
13. ☐ Answer it when it asks: which **country** to set (where you live), a **contact email** (optional — say "skip"
    if you don't want one public). If it asks for a password or code, *you* type it.
14. ☐ Leave that Chrome window alone while it works (~15–20 min). Use your phone for Phase 4.

## PHASE 4 — Meanwhile, on your phone (you, 10 min)
15. ☐ Install the **YouTube Studio** app and the **YouTube** app (if not already), sign in, switch to the
    *Hidden Engineering* channel in both.
16. ☐ Save the final video (**lock-short-v21.mp4**, I sent it) to your phone *and* your computer.
17. ☐ Rename the file to the title (YouTube reads the file name):
    **`Why a Burglar's Key Can't Open Your Door.mp4`**  (or the title you pick — see step 22).
18. ☐ (Optional, lets me read your results myself later) Open **vidiq.com** → sign in → **Connect YouTube channel** →
    pick *Hidden Engineering*.

## PHASE 5 — Check the extension's work (you, 3 min)
19. ☐ When the extension says it's done, read its ✅/❌ list. Anything ❌ → tell me, I'll fix the prompt.
20. ☐ Open **youtube.com/@yourhandle** on your **phone**: picture, banner text and description must look right and
    nothing cut off.

## PHASE 6 — Upload the Short (you, 10 min) — upload on the COMPUTER (more options), check on the PHONE
21. ☐ In YouTube Studio click **Create (top right) → Upload videos** → pick the renamed file.
22. ☐ **Title** (pick one; my first choice is first):
    - Why a Burglar's Key Can't Open Your Door
    - Why the Wrong Key Won't Turn (Inside a Lock)
    - The 0.7 mm That Keeps Burglars Out
23. ☐ **Description** (the default from setup is already there) — add on top:
    "A burglar is sneaking into your house in the middle of the night… but his key won't turn. Here's what's
    hiding inside every lock: 5 pairs of pins, springs, and a line that has to be perfect to within less than a
    millimetre."
24. ☐ **Playlist:** "How it works — Shorts". **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids".
25. ☐ **Show more → Altered content:** "No". Tags are pre-filled. **Category:** Science & Technology.
26. ☐ Click **Next** through *Video elements* (nothing to add for Shorts) to **Checks** → wait until
    **Copyright: No issues found** (green tick).
27. ☐ **Visibility:** leave it **Unlisted** → **Save**. Wait until it says **HD processed** (a few minutes).
28. ☐ Open the unlisted link on your **phone**: watch it once start to finish with sound. All good?
29. ☐ Pick the moment to go public: a weekday between **2 pm and 5 pm New York time** (US viewers are online after
    school/work). Studio → Content → the Short → **Visibility → Schedule** → that time → **Schedule**.
    (Or **Public** right away if it's already that time.)
30. ☐ Right after it goes public, post and **pin** this comment from the channel:
    "What should a burglar try next — the window, the garage, or the smart lock? 👀"
31. ☐ Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours (short, friendly). Don't ask friends to like/comment, never buy
    views — YouTube can spot it and it hurts the channel.

## PHASE 7 — After posting
32. ☐ 48 hours later: paste `POST_LAUNCH_ANALYSIS_PROMPT.md` into the extension (it only reads numbers) → send me
    its answer. Again at 7 days. (If you did step 18, just tell me "check the results" instead.)
33. ☐ When the first long video is out, set it as this Short's **Related video**: Studio → Content → Shorts → the
    Short → **Related video** → pick the long video → Save.

---

### What NOT to do
- Don't use auto-posting tools or bots, don't buy views/subscribers, don't upload the same video twice.
- Don't post more than 1–2 Shorts a day in the first weeks.
- Never share passwords, backup codes or phone codes with anyone (not even me — I never need them).
