# The guide — personality bible (read before writing any scene with him)

Look: lib/components/hero3d.js `{ build: 'stick' }` (curly-top hair, blue headphones, blue gear tee, jeans, white
sneakers). Name: _owner to decide_ (working name in scripts: "the guide").

## Who he is
- **Overconfident tinkerer.** He's sure he can figure anything out in 2 seconds — and is usually wrong first.
- **Deadpan.** When things go wrong he doesn't cry: he slowly turns and **stares at the camera**. This is his signature beat.
- **Sneaky / shortcut-taker.** Always tries the lazy or sneaky way first (wrong key, pushing the "pull" door, skipping steps).
- **Curious, not dumb.** Once the machine is explained he gets it, and his win is earned and a little cocky.
- **Never childish.** No cutesy bouncing or baby faces. Dry humour, timing and attitude carry the jokes.

## Signature moves (use 1–3 per video)
- **The deadpan stare:** freeze → slow head turn to camera (0.3 s) → half-lidded eyes, flat mouth → hold 0.5 s.
- **The suspicious sneak:** crouched tiptoe walk, head snapping left-right with the eyes leading.
- **The yank:** plants a foot on the machine and pulls with both hands, leaning way back, teeth gritted.
- **The headphone adjust:** after a win, taps/adjusts one headphone cup with a smug side-eye.
- **The double-take:** glance away → snap back with wide eyes.
- **The pratfall:** something gives way and he lands on his backside — then the deadpan stare.

## How he moves (rules)
- Every action: anticipation (wind-up opposite way) → action (fast) → overshoot → settle. Never a linear move.
- Hold poses: 0.2–0.5 s holds after big moments so the audience can read them. Stillness is funny.
- Eyes lead: the eyes move first, then the head, then the body (Hero3D.lagged handles head/arms delay).
- React a beat late (0.1–0.15 s) to surprises; freeze for 0.12–0.16 s before a big reaction.
- He's never fully still: breathing + weight shifts (Hero3D.human).
- Weight: feet plant, knees bend on landings, he leans against effort.

## Expressions he uses most
suspicious · deadpan · annoyed · determined · smug (confident) · panic · proud · oops.
