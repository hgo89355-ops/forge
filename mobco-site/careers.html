How we hire

Steps can vary by role and location.

  1. Apply

    Send your CV through the form below, or by email to the KSA or Egypt careers team.

  2. Review

    Our HR team reviews your profile against the needs of current and upcoming projects.

  3. First conversation

    If there’s a match, we contact you for a first call.

  4. Interviews

    Meet the hiring team to talk through your experience and the role.

  5. Offer & onboarding

    Successful candidates receive an offer and join the team.
