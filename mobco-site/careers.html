04Hiring journey

From application to day one

A clear, simple process. Exact steps can vary by role and location.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Send your CV through the form below, or by email to the KSA or Egypt careers team.

  2. 02

    Review

    Our HR team reviews your profile against the needs of current and upcoming projects.

  3. 03

    First conversation

    If your profile matches a need, we contact you to arrange an initial conversation.

  4. 04

    Interviews

    Meet the hiring team to discuss your experience, your projects and the role in more depth.

  5. 05

    Offer & onboarding

    Successful candidates receive an offer and are welcomed into the team.
