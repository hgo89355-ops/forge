# Content notes — subsidiary detail pages

Pages: `mobco-construction.html`, `mobco-developments.html`, `mobco-real-estate.html`
(shared assets: `assets/css/pages/subsidiary.css`, `assets/js/pages/subsidiary.js`).

All company copy is rendered **statically** in the HTML (English text + `data-ar*` Arabic) and was copied
verbatim from `assets/js/data/site-data.js` (`SUBSIDIARIES[].about / tagline / facts / short`, `REGIONS[].blurb`,
`PROJECTS[].summary / location / imageNote`). The pages were generated by a scratch script, so if the data
changes, the copy in the three HTML files must be updated to match.

## Verbatim client copy used
- **About** paragraphs: `SUBSIDIARIES[id].about` (EN + AR), unchanged.
- **Taglines**: "Elevating industry standards" · "Creating landmarks, defining excellence" · "Simplifying property management".
- **Pull quotes** repeat one sentence of the About text (marked as a decorative `figure`):
  - Construction: "Having completed 438 projects across four continents, our portfolio reflects a strong commitment to excellence and delivering exceptional results."
  - Developments: "Our mission is to create transformative developments that not only meet but surpass the highest industry standards." (sentence ends before ", leaving a significant…")
  - Real Estate: "This design allows us to tailor office environments to meet the unique needs of each tenant."
- **Key facts** strip: the four / three entries of `SUBSIDIARIES[].facts`, split into value + label for layout
  (e.g. "438 projects completed" → **438** / Projects completed; "Saudi Arabia · Canada · UK · Egypt" → **4** / Countries — …).
- **Hero lead**: `SUBSIDIARIES[].short`.
- **Construction footprint panels**: KSA, Canada and Egypt use `REGIONS[].blurb` verbatim.
- **Real Estate flagship lead**: "Modern office spaces designed with a common core that provides essential services, ensuring maximum efficiency and flexibility for our clients." (excerpt of the About paragraph).

## Discrepancies kept as stated (do not reconcile in copy — client to confirm)
- **438 projects** (Construction page) vs **430+** (home-page stats) vs **"more than 150 projects"** (About narrative).
  The Construction page shows 438 (counter) because it is the subsidiary's own figure.
- **"four continents"** (Construction text) vs **"three continents"** (group copy). The four *countries* named
  (Saudi Arabia, Canada, UK, Egypt) span three continents; the copy is left verbatim.
- Construction text was cut off on the client site after "delivering exceptional" — data adds "results" (see `SUBSIDIARIES.todo`).
- Real Estate text: minor grammar fix in data ("features an modern" → "features modern").
