About the company

MOBCO Real Estate Development

Established in 2002 in Cairo, Egypt, MOBCO Real Estate Development specializes in leasing and property management for multi-functional buildings. Our flagship project, Mivida Business Park, B1, features modern office spaces designed with a common core that provides essential services, ensuring maximum efficiency and flexibility for our clients. This design allows us to tailor office environments to meet the unique needs of each tenant.
