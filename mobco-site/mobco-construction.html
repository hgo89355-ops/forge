About the company

MOBCO Construction

Founded in 2001, MOBCO Construction, the construction arm of MOBCO Group, is a recognized leader in the industry, known for delivering innovative, high-quality projects. MOBCO Group operates across diverse sectors, including construction, development, education, hospitality, and facility management, consistently exceeding client expectations.

With tier-one status in the global construction industry, MOBCO's projects span Saudi Arabia, Canada, the UK, and Egypt. Having completed 438 projects across four continents, our portfolio reflects a strong commitment to excellence and delivering exceptional results.
