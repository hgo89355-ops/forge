    DUsage guidelines

    Recommended guidance — to be confirmed against the official brand book.

    • Do

      Use the navy logo on white or light backgrounds.

    • Do

      Use the white logo on navy, or on photography darkened for contrast.

    • Do

      Give the logo generous clear space, away from text and other graphics.

    • Don’t

      Don’t stretch, squash or rotate the logo.

    • Don’t

      Don’t recolour the logo or add shadows and effects.

    • Don’t

      Don’t place the logo on busy images without enough contrast.
